2. What data we collect

We only collect personal data when you voluntarily provide it through the contact form on the website:

We do not collect personal data automatically without your knowledge. If we introduce analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics) using cookies in the future, we will display a consent banner before they are activated.

3. Purpose and legal basis for processing

We process data submitted via the contact form to respond to your enquiry and prepare an offer — based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps necessary to enter into and perform a contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to customer enquiries).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. We do, however, use an external contact-form delivery service — FormSubmit — which technically forwards the submission content to our email address. It acts as a necessary data processor, working solely on our instructions and for the purpose of delivering your message.

5. Retention period

We retain correspondence data for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any resulting cooperation, and thereafter for the period required by law (e.g. tax regulations, if a business relationship followed).

6. Your rights

  • right of access to your data,
  • right to rectification,
  • right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"),
  • right to restriction of processing,
  • right to data portability,
  • right to object to processing,

To exercise the rights above, write to dpx369@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration, including contact-form safeguards against automated submissions (captcha, honeypot) and a site-wide Content Security Policy (CSP).

This policy may be updated periodically — e.g. following the introduction of new tools (analytics, CRM). The current version is always available on this page.
